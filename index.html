01 / Profile

Who is Samantha?

Kia ora, I'm Samantha Sison, although most people call me Sammy. I'm a Year 13 student and Deputy Head Girl at Wakatipu High School in Queenstown, New Zealand. I am planning to study a conjoint degree in Computer Science and Finance at the University of Auckland in 2027.

I am naturally curious and enjoy understanding how things work, especially when technology can be used to solve a practical problem. My interests sit at the intersection of technology, business, finance and community impact. Over the past few years, this has taken me from school technology projects and coding into an ICT internship at Skyline Enterprises, where I worked with a team to develop an AI-driven customer sentiment analysis web application.

Growing up in Queenstown has also shaped the way I think about opportunity. Regional communities are full of ambitious and talented young people, but access to professional networks and technology opportunities can be very different from what is available in larger cities. I care about finding ways to close that gap and create more opportunities for young people outside the main centres.

Outside of technology and academics, I am a very people-oriented person. I enjoy skiing, hiking, travelling, cooking, reading, photography, music and spending time with friends and whānau. I also love getting involved in school and community activities, whether that means volunteering, mentoring younger students, participating in sport or trying something completely new.

I am happiest when I am learning something new, surrounded by good people, or working towards something that has a purpose beyond myself.

02 / Journey

My Journey

My interests have developed through a mix of school, work, leadership, technology and opportunities outside Queenstown.

2024

Exploring Technology & Innovation

I began exploring technology beyond the classroom through GirlBossNZ and GirlsWhoGrowNZ. I researched applications of AI in healthcare, developed an energy-tracking app business model and co-led research into agricultural water filtration and irrigation.

2025

Technology, Community & Leadership

I expanded my involvement in technology and community projects, including volunteering with Whakatipu Youth Trust to improve website systems, sign-up processes and data organisation. I also continued tutoring mathematics and science and mentoring younger students through school programmes.

2025–26

Skyline ICT Summer Internship

I joined Skyline Enterprises as an ICT summer intern and worked in a team of four to develop an AI-driven customer sentiment analysis web application. I worked across data, APIs, web development and user experience, and presented our final solution to senior leadership and the Board.

2026

Deputy Head Girl

I became Deputy Head Girl at Wakatipu High School, supporting student initiatives, school-wide activities and student engagement while continuing my academic and community commitments.

2026

JBWere Impact100 Whakatipu

I was selected as the 2026 JBWere Impact100 Whakatipu recipient, working alongside local female leaders to assess charity proposals and contribute youth perspectives to the allocation of $188,000 in community grants.

2026

London International Youth Science Forum

I represented New Zealand and the Philippines at the London International Youth Science Forum at Imperial College London, connecting with students from around the world and exploring emerging science and technology with researchers and academics.

2027

Next Chapter

I am planning to move to Auckland to study a conjoint degree in Computer Science and Finance at the University of Auckland, continuing to explore how technology and financial thinking can be used to create meaningful impact.

03 / Projects

What I've Built

I enjoy projects where technology is more than just a task. I want what I build to have a practical use.

Skyline Sentiment Analysis

During my ICT internship at Skyline Enterprises, I worked in a team of four to develop an AI-driven customer review analysis web application. The system processes Google and TripAdvisor reviews, identifies sentiment and themes, and presents the results through an interactive dashboard.

EcoSmart Energy Tracker

Through GirlBossNZ, I developed a business model for an energy-tracking application designed to help households and businesses better understand their energy consumption and make more sustainable decisions.

GirlsWhoGrowNZ

I co-led a research project investigating agricultural water filtration and irrigation improvements for local Otago farmers, combining scientific research with a focus on practical environmental impact.

Whakatipu Youth Trust

I volunteer my technical skills with Whakatipu Youth Trust, helping maintain their website, improve sign-up systems, organise user information and connect website processes with Microsoft-based data systems.
